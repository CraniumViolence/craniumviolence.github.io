# Covens League Challenges
#### Introduction
This page covers all of the challenges for the **Covens of Umbra** league. 

While the guide makes the assumption that you have a baseline understanding of Path of Exile, it will still give fairly detailed information on even the simpler challenges.

Difficulties are subjective and are oriented around thoughts on doing them in a trade league, SSF is always going to be notably harder. If you are trying to complete challenges and this is your first league following a build guide is probably wise.

Mechanics related to the current league or new mechanics in general will naturally be covered more in depth. 

The guide is actively edited alongside the league progressing and people leaving feedback.

# 8 Atlas Additions
<div id="ch-8" markdown="8">
## Easy
<h2 class="req">5 of 8 required</h2>
[.ggg]Complete any five of the following League Mechanic encounters the specified number of times.
- [.ggg]Complete Ore Deposits x30
	- [.com]Get `Ore Deposits Chance` on the Atlas Tree
- [.ggg]Open Strongboxes x30
	- [.com]Get `Surpassing Chance of Strongboxes` on the Atlas Tree
- [.ggg]Defeat Mercenaries x30
	- [.com]Get `Mercenary Inhabited Chance` on the Atlas Tree
- [.ggg]Defeat Rogue Exiles x30
	- [.com]Get `Surpassing Chance of Rogue Exiles` on the Atlas Tree
- [.ggg]Activate Shrines x30
	- [.com]Get `Surpassing Chance of Shrines` on the Atlas Tree
- [.ggg]Defeat Monsters with Essences x30
	- [.com]Get `Surpassing Chance of Imprisoned Monsters` on the Atlas Tree
- [.ggg]Defeat Possessed Monsters x30
	- [.com]Take `Unrelenting Torment` on the Atlas Tree
	- [.com]Take `Exiled Will` on the Atlas Tree paired with Exile nodes
- [.ggg]Have Sacred Flora spread Wisps x30
	- [.com]This is just doing the new strongboxes I mean harvest
	- [.com]Take `Surpassing Chance of Sacred Flora` on the atlas tree
	
These are the 'sub-content' that you cannot force with threads. This makes each incredibly straight forward as you just use the tree.

Losing scarabs for these makes them bland and uninteresting. Enjoy up to five compulsory respecs I suppose.

Surpassing Chance adds additional spawns if you go over 100%. For example with 200% you get 2 a map, if you get 150% you get 1 and a chance of a second each map.

Normal Chance is a fixed rate that caps at 100%.

Increased Chance takes the base rate of something (usualy around 8%) and increases it.
</div>

# Basic Observations

There are a bunch of ruminations from the preview site here for little good reason.

There is an image from the preview site that does imply they were going to do per mechanic trees like PoE2 has I suppose. It would certainly have been better than the mess threads ended up!

They are here for posterity I suppose and <a href="#" onclick="$('.prthoughts').fadeToggle(600);return false;">you can click here</a> to see them even they are mostly worthless. 

[.prthoughts]A lot of changes are very likely to end up negative in terms of time efficiency for challenges.

[.prthoughts]`Scarabs` being dead and their power 'maybe' being on the tree or map enchants means that costs of swapping mechanics are going to rise astronomically as you will need a decent amount of tree respec points to force anything not available as a thread.
- [.prthoughts]Specifically threads are only for `Abyss`, `Blight`, `Legion`, `Breach`, `Incursion` (Alva), `Betrayal` (Jun), `Bestiary` (Einhar), `Expedition`, `Ultimatum` and `Ritual`

[.prthoughts]`Mirage`, `Sentinel` and `Wildwood` are all rollable on maps themselves directly (purely with chaos orbs; presumably scour-alch or scour-binding too) which might end up being a challenge which will naturally be a currency sink and if you aren't buying them will involve some delightfully boring regex crafting. Mirage and Wildwood maps seem especially likely to be valuable as well though so it's also possibly a good shovel strategy.

[.prthoughts]Similarly, the new `9-mod maps` are multiple players of RNG and are probably likely to be a challenge. Running Alva to get a single room type, to run a temple 2-3 maps later to then have a chance of getting a 9-mod map does not sound appealing, especially if you want to avoid buying them. Also similarly, good shovel strategy as you can sell temples or the 9-mod maps to people who want to do less monotonous tasks.

[.prthoughts]`Threads` in general are a large question mark in terms of how they turn out, I'm assuming weighted modifiers so if you need very specific combos for a challenge or similar they might suck a lot.

[.prthoughts]The `actual Coven league mechanic` itself looks to be a de facto bossing league including new drops being tied to uber bosses buffed by the league mechanic, this means there's a good chance they could have some actually hard challenges tied to bosses this time around. It also means carry services will be profitable and in demand which might make them more expensive than average for challenges too. 

[.prthoughts]Depending on the level of buffs bosses get the league might be one where you actually need something slightly less squishy than a dirt cheap miner but I would expect 12 septillion DPS will still outpace anything the mechanic adds short of it being 'immune to damage' conditionals.

[.prthoughts]There is (or was, seemingly, nothing much has been mentioned around synergies and they're not adding much new from the item filter info) a big focus on pushing what would previously have been cross contamination style interactions to the forefront, I would expect at least one challenge completely focused around all of the new interactions they are adding between leagues.

# PoE1Bad

These are more in-depth thoughts on why I didn't like how the endgame rework turned out. 

They are here mostly for catharsis but <a href="#" onclick="$('.egthoughts').fadeToggle(600);return false;">you can click here</a> to see them even if you won't get much from them! 

<h4 class="egthoughts">Scarabs and Threads and Astrolabes</h4>

[.egthoughts]People clapped and hollered their approval of this like they were on Jerry springer experiencing schadenfreude at the expense of someone discovering they are the father of an unwanted baby. 

[.egthoughts]But now we have *threads* which are absolutely abhorrent as a system. Everything about them is actively bad and removes every aspect of scarabs that I liked. No agency and no granularity is a terrible combination. I do not want to play gacha on being able to play the content in the way I previously could, they need to fuck right off with this absolute mess of a system.

[.egthoughts]Astrolabes don't have a reason to exist now, no idea why they made them baseline while also removing vault farming. It's not an approved farming strategy I guess.

[.egthoughts]Instead of strats we now play blackjack, they deal the cards from the deck and you get whatever you are given and you will like it or use your six defensive layers. Absolutely disgraceful showing.

[.egthoughts]Scarabs needed the 3 to 1 recipe removed, the chaff turned into something even slightly interesting and the ones that overperformed dialled back and made less rare so they would not be 300c when other things are 0.2c. It's just the most extreme option for the sake of it and it made everything worse and just removed strategies as an idea.

[.egthoughts]Threads are functionally the direct equivalent to playing with Twist of Fate with a set of scarabs that get randomised just with them respecting a pool limit and with difficulty scaling like you're pushing delve too hard. That this is the default and only vector of adding things beyond the anaemic tree is bleak.

<h4 class="egthoughts">Heist</h4>

[.egthoughts]Farming blueprints in maps was a wonderful supplementary mechanic. You could invest in Huck and get power from something you would otherwise block. Now you just never see them at all in any circumstances and it's functionally gone. Apparently the loot in heist is too so at least they too can be funnelled into running the few approved strategies with supplementary thread sprinkles of varying colours.

<h4 class="egthoughts">Beyond, Delirium</h4>

[.egthoughts]You can't force Delirium Mirrors or Beyond any more outside of rolling maps. But hey beyond is now opt in... what do you *mean* you could already do that by simply not taking the nodes to 100% or running maps with it..?

<h4 class="egthoughts">Map Rolling</h4>

[.egthoughts]Why is this a thing again. We could finally just make a regex and use async to buy maps we wanted off dozens of sellers farming them with the scarabs. Now we need to farm up temples? Who thought that was an equivalent exchange to the scarab?

<h4 class="egthoughts">Bestiary</h4>

[.egthoughts]Who thought the wildwood interactions were good ideas? I know they are thematically linked but that doesn't mean you connect the gameplay. And the datamined values are *2000 of all types*? Which absolute clown approved those numbers or the mod in general?

<h4 class="egthoughts">Harvest</h4>

[.egthoughts]Strongboxes but also Wildwood now! They're dialling it down according to the what we're working on post but it should never have existed as a default and is de facto the exact type of thing that should be opt in.

[.egthoughts]We lost one of the most interesting to optimise mechanics in the game with multiple ways to play it and constant moment to moment microdecisions that could lead to big pay offs (especially if you were doing Crop Rotation) and instead we now have... Strongboxes. You pray you get the right type, you roll them with currency and then you open them and kill mobs for your loot.

[.egthoughts]Please stop making leagues into strongboxes, the fact GGG are planning to bring back Allflame from their post about it will mean we get even more strongbox leagues in place of interesting ones. At least that one is making circles to path to them, harvest just gets 'bosses that break horribly in every map'.

<h4 class="egthoughts">Mirage</h4>

[.egthoughts]They are now Zana maps. A map in your map, every time. They're honestly exhausting instead of interesting, portals need to just work like they do in a real map so you can actually leave with every single one being oversized now. They genuinely need to work like Mirage League ones did instead and mirage maps should be brought back.

[.egthoughts]Not that you can plan your juice any more. Threads being awful and all.

<h4 class="egthoughts">Atlas Tree</h4>

[.egthoughts]It's a mix up for the sake of a mix up. It does not feel better designed, the nerfs mean it is actively less enabling and I vehemently dislike the forced routing through mechanics instead of them being paths you could take that were less point efficient but which gave you common desired mechanics without massively impacting your pathing. The Circle doesn't accomplish this.

[.egthoughts]As an Aside, the guide can technically show the new atlas and where points are, like this is the new Eater ring!

[.egthoughts]I didn't go in wanting to dislike anything, it's just actually quite bad. Even the imagery, why isn't there the void of space behind the slowly spinning map device visual that makes up the boring circle now? Really obvious miss.

[.egthoughts]The Atlas Tree being stale wasn't call for it and scarabs being taken out back. You have mechanics for the passive tree, you can use them
