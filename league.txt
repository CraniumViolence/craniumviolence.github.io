# Covens League Challenges
#### Introduction
This page covers all of the challenges for the **Covens of Umbra** league. 

While the guide makes the assumption that you have a baseline understanding of Path of Exile, it will still give fairly detailed information on even the simpler challenges.

Difficulties are subjective and are oriented around thoughts on doing them in a trade league, SSF is always going to be notably harder. If you are trying to complete challenges and this is your first league following a build guide is probably wise.

Mechanics related to the current league or new mechanics in general will naturally be covered more in depth. 

The guide is actively edited alongside the league progressing and people leaving feedback.

# Basic Observations
A lot of changes are very likely to end up negative in terms of time efficiency for challenges.

`Scarabs` being dead and their power 'maybe' being on the tree or map enchants means that costs of swapping mechanics are going to rise astronomically as you will need a decent amount of tree respec points to force anything not available as a thread.
- Specifically threads are only for `Abyss`, `Blight`, `Legion`, `Breach`, `Incursion` (Alva), `Betrayal` (Jun), `Bestiary` (Einhar), `Expedition`, `Ultimatum` and `Ritual`

`Mirage`, `Sentinel` and `Wildwood` are all rollable on maps themselves directly (purely with chaos orbs; presumably scour-alch or scour-binding too) which might end up being a challenge which will naturally be a currency sink and if you aren't buying them will involve some delightfully boring regex crafting. Mirage and Wildwood maps seem especially likely to be valuable as well though so it's also possibly a good shovel strategy.

Similarly, the new `9-mod maps` are multiple players of RNG and are probably likely to be a challenge. Running Alva to get a single room type, to run a temple 2-3 maps later to then have a chance of getting a 9-mod map does not sound appealing, especially if you want to avoid buying them. Also similarly, good shovel strategy as you can sell temples or the 9-mod maps to people who want to do less monotonous tasks.

`Threads` in general are a large question mark in terms of how they turn out, I'm assuming weighted modifiers so if you need very specific combos for a challenge or similar they might suck a lot.

The `actual Coven league mechanic` itself looks to be a de facto bossing league including new drops being tied to uber bosses buffed by the league mechanic, this means there's a good chance they could have some actually hard challenges tied to bosses this time around. It also means carry services will be profitable and in demand which might make them more expensive than average for challenges too. 

Depending on the level of buffs bosses get the league might be one where you actually need something slightly less squishy than a dirt cheap miner but I would expect 12 septillion DPS will still outpace anything the mechanic adds short of it being 'immune to damage' conditionals.

There's a big focus on pushing what would previously have been cross contamination style interactions to the forefront, I would expect at least one challenge completely focused around all of the new interactions they are adding between leagues.

Sceptical on the changes as it stands and the patch notes didn't encourage me much. Hopefully it feels better than the patch notes read to me and the challenge rewards are good enough to justify playing.
