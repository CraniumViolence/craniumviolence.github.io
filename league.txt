# Allflame League Challenges
#### Introduction
This page covers all of the challenges for the **Curse of the Allflame** league. 

While the guide makes the assumption that you have a baseline understanding of Path of Exile, it will still give fairly detailed information on even the simpler challenges.

Difficulties are subjective and are oriented around thoughts on doing them in a trade league, SSF is always going to be notably harder. If you are trying to complete challenges and this is your first league following a build guide is probably wise.

Mechanics related to the current league or new mechanics in general will naturally be covered more in depth. 

The guide is actively edited alongside the league progressing and people leaving feedback.

[.centeritalic]If you want to see my thoughts on Curse of the Allflame, <a href="#" onclick="$('.lthoughts').fadeToggle(600);return false;">you can click here</a>. 

<h4 class="lthoughts">Thoughts on the League</h4>

<h5 class="lthoughts">General State</h5>

[.lthoughts]The general state of the game is worse than last league. Raw drops feel bad and they seem to have an unhealthy attraction to sticking everything into boxes like some kind of demented Amazon warehouse. We need more Wish warehouses, GGG, loot belongs on the floor not in boxes!

[.lthoughts]Breach especially feels awful now. It still gives some amount of loot and is still useful but it feels absolutely anaemic even with a full tree and scarab set monster density wise.

<h5 class="lthoughts">Abyss</h5>

[.lthoughts]The Abyss rework is a fascinating combination of 'loot drought' and 'over abundance'. Running the actual Abysses themselves gives you basically nothing and then GGG also left the b{Abyss Scarab of Descending} in place, which murders prices on everything in there.

[.lthoughts]The changes in the last patches made only a very minor difference, sadly. Abysses themselves still suck though Abyssal Depths are slightly better at least.

<h5 class="lthoughts">Legion</h5>

[.lthoughts]Legion changes were nothing. They just removed incubators and changed the X-way layout a bit. Enshrouded things are cool but they are hardly a 'rework'.

<h5 class="lthoughts">Allflame League Mechanics</h5>

[.lthoughts]The actual league mechanic is very badly executed in general.

[.lthoughts]The maps component is 'click a box so you can kill things to click a box. They literally invented 'worse strongboxes'.

[.lthoughts]The charts should just done while mapping, either Mirage style in an instance of Harvest style in a side zone. There's slim benefit to them being itemised and it makes the flow worse.

[.lthoughts]The initial buffs seem to have rounded it out a bit (the Gems chests existing are a nerf, frankly) but it's a lot of complexity for the sake of complexity in the same way betrayal is. Mapping grids and reading borders you can't see is just tedium.

<h5 class="lthoughts">The Servers</h5>

[.lthoughts]Being trapped in Europe I usually have a dire start to the league but they were mostly good this time.

[.lthoughts]As of typing, there's been multiple full instance crashes with up to a minute of rollback each time. It hasn't felt great!

# 9 Ingrained Increases
<div id="ch-9" markdown="9">
## Very Easy
<h2 class="req">3 of 6 required</h2>
[.ggg]Complete any three of the following League Mechanic encounters the specified number of times.
- [.ggg]Defeat Monsters with Essence (30x)
	- [.com]Atlas points can guarantee at least **1** monster each map
	- [.com]b{Essence Scarab} adds **2** monsters up to a max of **10** while using five
	- [.com]b{Essence Scarab of Calcification} converts **all rare monsters** in a map to be imprisoned
- [.ggg]Open Strongboxes (30x)
	- [.com]Atlas points can guarantee at least **1** strongbox each map
	- [.com]b{Ambush Scarab} will add **4** to a map up to a max of **12** while using three
- [.ggg]Activate Shrines (30x)
	- [.com]Atlas points can guarantee at least **1** shrine a map
	- [.com]b{Domination Scarab} adds **2** shrines to a map up to a max of **8** while using four
	- [.com]b{Domination Scarab of Apparitions} adds **2** Apparition Shrines to a map
	- [.com]b{Domination Scarab of Evolution} adds **1** Evolving Shrine up to a maximum of **2** while using two
- [.ggg]Defeat Possessed Monsters (30x)
	- [.com]The league `Cursed Treasure` spawns monsters that are possessed and gives you a buff after using it, you are likely to have cleared this by chance if you engage with those
	- [.com]Atlas points can add at least **1** Tormented Spirit
		- [.com]Pairing this with `Rogue Exiles` is sensible as `Exiled Will` causes Rogue Exiles to be possessed
	- [.com]b{Torment Scarab of Possession} grants a fixed 25% chance for each rare in an area to be possessed up to a maximum of 75%
	- [.com]b{Torment Scarab} adds **3** additional Tormented Sprits to an area
	- [.com]Do not take `Speaker of the Dead` if you want to complete this
	- [.com]This is an obvious skip because it's awkward compared to the rest
- [.ggg]Defeat Rogue Exiles (30x)
	- [.com]Atlas points can add **1** Rogue Exile and can cause them to appear in pairs or spawn extras with a map boss
	- [.com]b{Anarchy Scarab} adds **4** Rogue Exiles up to a maximum of **20** while using five
	- [.com]b{Anarchy Scarab of Partnership} is a 50% chance for Rogue Exiles to appear in pairs
- [.ggg]Defeat Rare Beyond Monsters (30x)
	- [.com]A b{Beyond Scarab} and the Atlas can all enable Beyond monsters spawning
	- [.com]Atlas points can lower requirements for spawning monsters and `Endless Tide` makes it so you cannot spawn bosses (bosses stop further beyond monster spawns by default)
	- [.com]b{Beyond Scarab of Haemophilia} and b{Beyond Scarab of the Invasion} both make spawning Beyond monsters easier
	
Nothing terribly complex, interact with some past league mechanics in a moderate amount.
</div>

# 10 Legendary Leagues
<div id="ch-10" markdown="10">
## Very Easy
<h2 class="req">5 of 12 required</h2>
[.ggg]Complete any five of the following League Mechanic encounters the specified number of times.
- [.ggg]Complete Abysses (30x)
	- [.com]Abyss was reworked and it's simpler than it was, go near the big glowing green nodes and kill things until they turn into a chest or grey to indicate they have no reward
	- [.com]b{Abyss Scarab} adds **1** and has a limit of **5**
	- [.com]`Underground Kingdom` on the Atlas Passive Tree adds increased chance for Abysses based on pack size and it otherwise has fixed percentage chance nodes.
	- [.com]You can use a b{Lightless Astrolabe} to get additional Abysses and related mods
- [.ggg]Complete Expeditions (30x)
	- [.com]Requires reading to avoid bricking encounters, truly not for the faint of heart
	- [.com]b{Expedition Scarab} adds an expedition to a map or you can force it with various atlas points
	- [.com]You can use a b{Runic Astrolabe} to get expedition related mods but they will not make this faster
- [.ggg]Harvest Plots (100x)
	- [.com]You will get multiple plots per harvest making this about equal to everything else
	- [.com]b{Harvest Scarab} adds an expedition to a map or you can force it with various atlas points
	- [.com]You can use a b{Fruiting Astrolabe} to get Harvest related mods but they will not make this faster
- [.ggg]Complete Legion Encounters (30x)
	- [.com]As many per map as you push onto it
	- [.com]The Atlas Passive Tree forces one minimum
	- [.com]You can force up to five using a set of five b{Legion Scarab}
	- [.com]You can use a b{Timeless Astrolabe} to get additional Legion encounters and related mods
- [.ggg]Open Smuggler's Caches (30x)
	- [.com]Very unobtrusive, simply click the caches as you see them while doing anything else
	- [.com]The Atlas Passive Tree allows you to force a minimum of one cache with potential for up to eight
- [.ggg]Pass through Mirrors of Delirium (30x)
	- [.com]One a map maximum
	- [.com]b{Delirium Scarab} adds a mirror to a map
	- [.com]One a map, you can immediately end it because it's just passing through
	- [.com]You can use a b{Deceptive Astrolabe} to get Delirium related mods but they will not make this faster
- [.ggg]Complete Ultimatum Waves (200x)
	- [.com]One a map maximum
	- [.com]b{Ultimatum Scarab} adds an Ultimatum to a map
	- [.com]You can get 10 to 13 waves per Ultimatum, this seems balanced around full clearing but you can chip away at it with short runs too
	- [.com]You can use a b{Chaotic Astrolabe} to get Ultimatum related mods but they will not make this faster
- [.ggg]Complete Blights (30x)
	- [.com]One a map maximum
	- [.com]b{Blight Scarab} adds a blight to a map
	- [.com]Can be forced with the Atlas Passive Tree or a b{Blight Scarab}
	- [.com]You can use a b{Fungal Astrolabe} to get Blight related mods but they will not make this faster
- [.ggg]Complete Rituals (100x)
	- [.com]3 or 4 per map
	- [.com]b{Ritual Scarab} adds rituals to a map
	- [.com]You can use a b{Nameless Astrolabe} to get Ritual related mods but they will not make this faster
- [.ggg]Complete Ore Deposit Encounters (30x)
	- [.com]As many per map as you push onto it
	- [.com]b{Kalguuran Scarab} adds two to a map	
	- [.com]The Atlas Passive Tree can force ore in maps
	- [.com]You can use up to two b{Kalguuran Scarab} to get four extra deposits on a map
- [.ggg]Complete Unstable Breaches or Hives (30x)
	- [.com]b{Breach Scarab of Instability} adds two to a map and is limited to two
	- [.com]As many per map as you push onto it, one to five deterministically
	- [.com]Using a full Breach tree with two b{Breach Scarab of Instability} is recommended and can get a lot per map
	- [.com]Hives are slower but give more Hiveblood
- [.ggg]Defeat Mercenaries (30x)
	- [.com]The return of the Mercenary mechanic, Luminaries will get this one as a freebie!
	- [.com]b{Trarthan Scarab} adds a Mercenary to an area
	- [.com]b{Trarthan Scarab of Surprising Alliances} can have Wild Mercenaries accompany Rogue Exiles but **I have not verified if this counts for completion**.
</div>

		- [.com]Remnants are the things that buff monsters and give reward changes, you need 10 total active by the final explosion
		- [.com]Hovering the icon in the middle right of your screen will give you a count of how many you tagged as `Remnants tagged by placed Explosives: X/X`

- [.ggg]Domains of Timeless Conflict with at least 25 rewards (3x)
	- [.com]Ironically, this is harder to see now they changed it to feel 'less like an arcade game'.
	- [.com]Can be done in a four or five-way fairly easily as long as your build is decent at clearing, simply get the emblems
	- [.com]Buying in to a five-way rota will obviously complete this for you while also levelling you but is a lot more than simply buying the emblems if your character can handle it
	- [.com]This might sound hard if you are unfamiliar with the content, but it is legitimately not even a single full row of rewards and it does not take a notable build to reach this amount

	- [.com]Requires engaging with Heist as all the blueprint will require that you have levelled up your Rogues to be taken

# Easing Long Term Grind
The biggest thing this league realistically seems to just be 'do not neglect doing your charts and voyages'.

The league mechanic is overall pretty poor incorporated into the core game in terms of flow, so it is very easy to just use the mechanic it adds to your map and keep going without ever going to the boat.

Naturally this means when you want to actually start planning a voyage, you have to do nine charts of chores to unlock your nine voyage components and it quickly snowballs into a lot of monotony and tedium.

# Challenges to Skip
You get the full set of challenge rewards at **36/40** completion, which allows you to skip up to four different challenges.

The biggest, most obvious skips are:
- `Horrific Halls`
	- Very juiced, 100% delirious nightmare maps are simply not feasible on a lot of builds
- `Apex Assailants`
	- Uber Bossing is Uber Bossing. Obvious skip if you don't do that in a league or don't want to buy them.
- `Brinerot's Bloody Bounty`
	- This is here tentatively until I actually know what it involves, conditionals are either very awful or very free with little inbetween I find.
- `Grinding Gallant Goals`
	- Grindy but not terribly notable or hard beyond that
- `Astounding Astrolabes`
	- As above, so below

Generally speaking this league continues the trend of being fairly easy with a curve ball for those wanting 40/40 in the form of Horrific Halls.

# Unlocks
This league finally has some spice to it and isn't just a full set of armour! This time around there's a Weapon Effect, Pet, Back Attachment and Portal.
- At 12 challenge completion you can unlock the Oceanic Weapon Effect.
- At 24 challenge completion you can unlock the Fangtooh Moray Pet.
- At 32 challenge completion you can unlock the Captain's Wheel Back Attachment.
- At 36 challenge completion you can unlock the Oceanic Portal Effect.

You can view [a full preview video is available on the official Path of Exile YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5_05mNyIg-c) for the rewards.
